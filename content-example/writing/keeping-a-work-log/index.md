At the end of every working day I write three lines in a plain text file: what I did, what got in the way and what comes next.

## Why it helps

It takes two minutes, and it pays off in three ways. Mornings start faster, because yesterday's last line tells me where to begin. Reviews are easier, because the year is already written down. And patterns show up: the same blocker three weeks in a row is worth raising.

## The format

There is no app and no template beyond a date heading:

```md title="log.md"
## 2026-05-04

- Did: moved the settings page to server rendering.
- Blocked: waiting on the new icons.
- Next: write tests for the date picker.
```

The habit matters more than the tool. A notebook works just as well.
